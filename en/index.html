It starts with a conversation.

No training and no manual. If you can send a voice note, you can use Myto.

Step 1

Tell Myto about your place

It asks about ten questions to begin. Type or just talk. It learns the rest as it goes, one question at a time, until it knows your business the way a good manager would.

Step 2

Connect what you already use

Your WhatsApp Business number, your Google reviews, a photo of your menu. We do the setup with you. Nothing to connect? A photo or a voice note is enough.

Step 3

Myto gets to work. You approve.

At first it asks before every new kind of action. Once you trust how it handles something, you let it handle that on its own.

    Questions owners ask
    
  
    
    
    Do I need to be good with technology?
    No. If you can send a voice note, you can use Myto. We set it up with you and stay close during the first weeks.
    
    
    What do my customers see?
    They write to your usual WhatsApp number and get answers in your business's name and tone. Your team can still reply from the same phone at any time.
    
    
    What if Myto gets something wrong?
    Anything new needs your OK, and when Myto isn't sure it asks you. You can pause it with one switch and take over the conversation yourself.
    
    
    Does it replace my staff?
    No. It takes the repetitive messages so your team can look after the people in front of them.
    
    
    Which languages does it speak?
    Darija, French, Arabic and English today. More languages are planned for riads and hotels.
    
    
    Is it only for restaurants?
    Restaurants and cafés come first, because that is where Myto was born. Riads and hotels are next.
